# LIGHT Internal Request
## TR-09 — Revision, Cancellation, Reversal, and Audit

- Product: LIGHT Internal Request
- Target Platform: Odoo 18
- Baseline Version: 1.0
- Status: BASELINED / LOCKED

## Purpose

This document defines historical correction and structured audit behavior.

## Core Principle

Historical business facts shall not be silently rewritten.

Different correction concepts have different meanings:

- Revision
- Cancellation
- Reversal
- Supersede

They shall not be collapsed into one generic "edit history" mechanism.

## Revision

Revision applies to material changes to an existing business requirement after
submission.

Examples:

- quantity
- Product
- significant specification
- financial basis
- fulfillment requirement

Canonical model:

`light.ir.revision`

Revision shall preserve applicable before/after context.

## Revision Impact

A Revision may trigger:

- Need Reapproval
- Financial Reapproval
- Fulfillment Replanning
- downstream conflict resolution

## Company

Post-submission Company change is not an ordinary V1 Revision.

Company change shall require the applicable controlled cancellation/new IR
business process.

## Cancellation

Cancellation removes remaining authorized business requirement through an
authorized business event.

Canonical model:

`light.ir.cancellation`

Cancellation shall preserve:

- IR / Line
- quantity
- reason
- actor
- timestamp
- authorization context
- Company

Cancellation does not erase prior authorization or execution history.

## Reversal

Reversal corrects a finalized historical event while preserving the original.

Example:

Acceptance +10
Reversal -2

The +10 event remains historical.

## Supersede

Supersede applies where a new governance cycle/version replaces an earlier
cycle for future business effect.

Earlier records remain historical.

## No Generic Reopen

V1 shall not implement unrestricted:

`Reopen IR`

A terminal transaction may only regain active work through an explicitly
defined controlled business event.

## Structured Audit

Critical events shall use structured records.

Chatter may additionally record user-readable messages.

Chatter is not the sole authoritative source.

## Audit Actor

Critical actor identity shall be determined from authenticated server-side
context.

Client input shall not be trusted as authoritative actor identity.

## Audit Timestamp

Critical timestamps shall be generated by the server/database/Odoo transaction
context.

## Audit Reason

Where a reason is required, it shall be persisted structurally rather than
only in a chatter message.

---

## TR-AUD-001 — Revision Preserves Prior State

Material Revision shall preserve sufficient prior business state for audit and
traceability.

## TR-AUD-002 — Reapproval Uses New Cycle

Where Revision requires reapproval, the implementation shall create an
applicable new Approval Cycle rather than rewriting a finalized cycle.

## TR-AUD-003 — Cancellation Is Structured

Cancellation shall use structured auditable records/events.

## TR-AUD-004 — Reversal Is Additive History

Historical correction by Reversal shall add a corrective event rather than
deleting or overwriting the original finalized event.

## TR-AUD-005 — No Generic Reopen

No unrestricted generic Reopen operation shall be provided.

## TR-AUD-006 — Critical Actor Is Server Determined

Critical historical actor fields shall be populated from trusted server-side
business context.

## TR-AUD-007 — Critical Timestamp Is Server Determined

Critical event timestamps shall not rely on arbitrary client-supplied values.

## TR-AUD-008 — Chatter Is Supplementary

Odoo Chatter shall be supplementary and shall not replace required structured
critical audit records.

## TR-AUD-009 — Historical Delete Protection

Finalized governance/business history shall be protected against ordinary hard
delete.

## TR-AUD-010 — Audit Traceability

Authorized users shall be able to trace applicable history from IR through
approval, fulfillment, downstream execution, Acceptance, Exception,
Cancellation, and Revision.

## TR-AUD-011 — Company Change Is Not Normal Revision

Post-submission Company change shall be blocked as an ordinary Revision in V1.

## TR-AUD-012 — Audit Reason Required Where Applicable

Revision, Cancellation, Rejection, Exception, Reversal, and other designated
critical actions shall preserve an applicable business reason.

---

## Audit Minimum

For applicable critical events preserve:

- object/reference
- Company
- actor
- timestamp
- event/action
- quantity/amount basis where relevant
- reason where required
- authority/reference where relevant
- source event/document where relevant

## Revision/Audit Baseline Statement

`TR-AUD-001` through `TR-AUD-012` constitute the V1 Revision and Audit
baseline.
